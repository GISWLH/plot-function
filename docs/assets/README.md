# Gallery data and artwork

## Computed figures

`global-temperature.png`, `seasons.png`, `seasonal-contrast.png`, and
`east-asia.png` are generated by `examples/gallery.py`. They are actual computed
plots, not image-generated depictions of data.

- **Input:** `data/ERA5temp_1978_monthly.nc`, distributed with the original
  repository. Its metadata identifies an ERA5/ECMWF GRIB-to-NetCDF conversion;
  the `t2m` field has 12 monthly time steps in 1978, a 0.25° grid, and Kelvin units.
- **Reference:** [ERA5 monthly averaged data on single levels](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/reanalysis-era5-single-levels-monthly-means).
  The repository does not contain the original data retrieval request. These
  examples use the bundled file, not a new download or a newly validated dataset.
- **Units:** subtract 273.15 for Celsius; temperature differences use the same
  numerical scale in Kelvin and Celsius.
- **Annual panel:** unweighted arithmetic mean of the 12 monthly fields. This is
  not weighted by the number of days in each month.
- **Comparison panels:** January and July monthly means with identical limits.
- **Difference panel:** July minus January within 1978. It is not a climate trend,
  future scenario, or anomaly relative to a multi-year climatology.
- **Display sampling:** every fourth latitude and longitude point, yielding 1°
  spacing. No interpolation or smoothing of the source array. Filled contours
  and isotherms in the regional figure are drawn by Matplotlib.
- **Basemap:** [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) public-domain
  110m coastline geometry, rendered by Cartopy. It is a visual reference layer,
  not part of the NetCDF input requirement. Use `--no-coastlines` to omit it.

The code's MIT license does not replace the applicable terms of source datasets.

## Decorative branding

`header.png` and `logo.png` are AI-generated decorative artwork created for this
project. They are separate assets, both designed on white backgrounds; the header
does not embed the standalone logo. The abstract contour shapes are not observations, model outputs, or
geographic boundaries. They are deliberately separate from the computed gallery.

## Reproduction

From an installed checkout:

```bash
python examples/gallery.py
python examples/gallery.py --no-coastlines --output examples/output/gallery
```

The second command writes separate offline outputs and does not overwrite these
checked-in images. The lightweight `examples/quickstart.py` creates a synthetic,
analytical field and explicitly labels it as synthetic.

## Research compositions (0.3)

Generated by `examples/journal_gallery.py`:

- `journal-global.png`: real bundled ERA5 annual monthly-mean average, a zonal
  mean ±1 population spatial SD, and a cosine-latitude weighted histogram.
- `journal-regional.png`: real July 1978 ERA5 field over 92–142°E, 8–53°N; an
  equal-cell zonal mean, meridional median/IQR, weighted histogram, and Natural
  Earth 50m rivers, lakes, borders, and coastlines. Summaries use cell centers
  inside the geographic rectangle.
- `journal-significance.png`: **synthetic**, not ERA5. Seed 20261009, 40 independent
  Gaussian realizations, known σ=2; two-sided zero-mean z-tests and BH FDR at 0.05
  across 4,331 cells. Stippling is visually thinned; hatching and contours use the
  complete same decision mask. Side ribbons show spatial SD, not confidence
  intervals. The NetCDF input is reproducibly generated under `examples/output/`.

The synthetic NetCDF is intentionally ignored by Git; rerun the script to create
it. All three images are computed Matplotlib figures on white backgrounds.
